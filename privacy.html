Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

1. Who we are

Mile Gazette is an editorial publication covering travel, destinations, and lifestyle stories. This policy explains what information we collect when you use Mile Gazette, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

When you subscribe to our newsletter or contact us, we collect the information you provide, such as your email address and any message content.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit Mile Gazette, we may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referral source. We use this to measure traffic, improve the site, and protect against abuse.

3. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send you the newsletter you requested;
  • Respond to your inquiries;
  • Measure and improve our content and site performance;
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, we process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — where you subscribe to the newsletter, you consent to us processing your email address for that purpose. You can withdraw consent at any time using the unsubscribe link in every email.
  • Legitimate interests — for site analytics, security, and improvement, where our interests do not override your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose data to comply with law.

5. Cookies

Mile Gazette uses only essential cookies necessary for the site to function. If we use analytics or advertising cookies in the future, we will ask for your consent before setting them and update this policy.

6. Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us operate the site (such as hosting and email delivery providers), who are bound by data protection obligations. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

7. Retention

We keep newsletter subscriber data until you unsubscribe, and contact inquiries for as long as needed to resolve them. Technical logs are retained for a limited period for security purposes.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, and to object to processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below.

10. Children

Mile Gazette is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.

12. Contact
