About

Better travel starts with clearer math.

Mile Gazette is an independent editorial guide to travel rewards. We turn complicated program rules, card benefits and award options into practical decisions.

What we cover

Our work focuses on card strategy, point valuations, award-booking tactics and destination planning. We favor worked examples, transparent assumptions and tools readers can use for themselves.

What we do not do

We do not promise guaranteed approvals, fixed award availability or universal point values. Travel programs and financial products change frequently; readers should confirm current terms with the provider.

How the site is funded

Mile Gazette may eventually earn compensation from selected partner links. Compensation will never determine a rating or prevent us from describing a product’s drawbacks. Pages containing compensated links will carry a clear disclosure.
