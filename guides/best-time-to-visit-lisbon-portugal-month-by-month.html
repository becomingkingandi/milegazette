Best Time to Visit Lisbon: Month-by-Month Breakdown for 2025

From sunny beach days to festive Christmas markets, here’s exactly when to go—and when to avoid the crowds.

5 min read Guides

The Short Answer: May and September Win

If you want warm weather, fewer crowds, and reasonable prices, book Lisbon for May or September. Daytime highs sit around 24–27°C (75–81°F), and you’ll get 10–11 hours of sunshine. Hotels run 20–30% cheaper than July and August, and you can still swim at nearby beaches like Cascais or Costa da Caparica. We’ve done both months multiple times—these are the sweet spots.

June to August: Peak Heat and Festivals

Summer in Lisbon is lively but hot. July and August average 28–30°C (82–86°F), and the city fills with tourists. Prices spike—a standard double in the Baixa district jumps from €120 to €200+ per night. The upside? The Feast of St. Anthony (June 12–13) turns the Alfama district into a giant street party with sardines and music. If you go in August, escape the heat with a day trip to Sintra’s cooler hills or the beaches at Guincho.

October and November: Mild but Rainy

Autumn brings cooler days (18–22°C / 64–72°F) and more rain—November gets around 120mm, the wettest month. But you’ll find fewer tourists and lower prices. We stayed in a Chiado apartment for €85/night in late October, half the summer rate. Pack a light rain jacket and plan indoor activities like the National Tile Museum or a fado night in Mouraria.

December to February: Quiet and Festive

Winter is the cheapest time to visit Lisbon. Flights from the US often drop 30–40% compared to summer, and hotels in the historic center start around €70–90/night. Temperatures hover at 14–16°C (57–61°F) with occasional sunny days. Christmas lights along Avenida da Liberdade and the December 1st independence celebrations add charm. Just note that many beachfront restaurants close until March.

March and April: Spring Bloom and Easter Crowds

Spring arrives early—by late March, almond trees are in bloom and temperatures hit 17–20°C (63–68°F). April brings Easter processions and a spike in visitors during the holiday week. Book at least 6–8 weeks ahead if you’re coming then. Otherwise, you’ll enjoy mild weather and manageable crowds. The Miradouro da Senhora do Monte offers stunning views without the summer crush.

Month-by-Month Weather at a Glance

Here’s a quick snapshot of average highs and rain days per month: Jan (14°C, 11 rain days), Feb (15°C, 9), Mar (17°C, 9), Apr (19°C, 9), May (22°C, 7), Jun (26°C, 3), Jul (28°C, 1), Aug (29°C, 2), Sep (26°C, 5), Oct (22°C, 10), Nov (18°C, 11), Dec (15°C, 12). Use this to match your travel style—sun worshippers go July–September; budget travelers aim for November–February.

Final Tips for Booking

For the best deals, book flights 2–3 months ahead and aim for mid-week departures—Tuesdays and Wednesdays are often 15–20% cheaper than weekends. Use a travel credit card with no foreign transaction fees, like the Chase Sapphire Preferred, to save on dining and transit. And always check the local events calendar—the Web Summit in early November packs hotels, while the Lisbon Fish and Flavours festival in April is a foodie bonus.
