When to Visit Lisbon: Month-by-Month Guide to Weather, Crowds, and Prices

From sunny beach days to festive winter lights, here's exactly when to book your Lisbon escape for the best weather, lowest prices, and fewest crowds.

5 min read Guides

Why Timing Matters in Lisbon

Lisbon is a year-round destination, but each season brings a different vibe. Summer (June–August) means packed miradouros and beach crowds, while winter (December–February) offers cooler days and cozy fado nights. Your ideal time depends on whether you want to bake on the sand or explore museums without queues. I've visited in every season—here's the honest breakdown.

Spring (March–May): The Sweet Spot

Spring is my top pick. Temperatures hover between 15°C and 22°C (59–71°F), and the city blooms with jacaranda trees. You'll find fewer tourists than summer, and hotel rates drop about 20% compared to July. Easter week gets busy, but late April and May are perfect for day trips to Sintra or Cascais. Pack a light jacket for evenings.

Summer (June–August): Sun, Festivals, and Crowds

Summer is hot—often 30°C (86°F) or more—and the city buzzes with festivals like Santo António (June 12) and the Festas de Lisboa. Beaches like Carcavelos and Costa da Caparica are packed, and prices peak. A mid-range hotel that costs €100 in April jumps to €180 in August. Book at least 3 months ahead if you must go then. Mornings are best for sightseeing before the heat hits.

Autumn (September–November): Warmth Without the Rush

September is a hidden gem—ocean water stays warm (around 20°C/68°F) and crowds thin out. October brings mild days and occasional rain, but you'll snag deals on flights and hotels. November is cooler and quieter, perfect for hitting the Museu Nacional de Arte Antiga or catching a fado performance in Alfama. Prices drop about 30% from summer highs.

Winter (December–February): Festive and Affordable

Winter is chilly but charming. December transforms the city with lights and Christmas markets, especially around Praça do Comércio. January and February are the cheapest months—flights from the US can drop to $400 round-trip, and hotels start at €60 a night. Rain is possible, but sunny days still hit 15°C (59°F). Pack layers and enjoy the local pasteis de nata without queues.

Month-by-Month Quick Picks

Here's a cheat sheet: March for mild weather and lower prices; June for festivals (book early); September for beach time without the crush; December for holiday vibes and budget stays. Avoid August if you dislike crowds and heat. For the best balance of good weather and value, aim for late April or early October—my personal favorites.

Final Tips for Booking

Use fare alerts on Google Flights to catch deals for shoulder seasons. If you're flexible, midweek flights are cheaper—depart on Tuesday or Wednesday. For hotels, look in Graça or Alfama for charm, or Baixa for convenience. Always check if your credit card covers travel insurance—saves you €50–100. And remember: Lisbon's hills mean comfy shoes are non-negotiable, no matter when you visit.
