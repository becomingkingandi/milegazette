When to Visit Bangkok: A Month-by-Month Guide to Weather, Crowds, and Deals

Bangkok is a year-round destination, but the right month can save you money and keep you out of the rain.

5 min read Guides

The Short Answer

The best time to visit Bangkok is from November to February. During these months, the weather is cooler and drier, with temperatures averaging around 77°F (25°C). Hotel prices are higher, but you'll avoid the heavy rains and sweltering heat. If you're on a budget, consider March to May — it's hot but less crowded, and room rates drop by 20-30%.

Cool Season (November to February)

This is Bangkok's peak tourist season. Expect clear skies, low humidity, and temperatures that rarely exceed 90°F (32°C). It's perfect for exploring temples like Wat Arun or taking a boat ride on the Chao Phraya River. Book accommodations at least 2-3 months ahead. A mid-range hotel room that costs $50 in September might run $70-80 here.

Hot Season (March to May)

March through May is scorching, with April often hitting 100°F (38°C). The upside? Fewer tourists and cheaper flights. You'll also catch Songkran, the Thai New Year water festival, from April 13-15. It's a massive street party where everyone gets soaked. If you don't mind the heat, this is a great time to snag a 4-star hotel for under $60 a night.

Rainy Season (June to October)

Rainy season runs from June to October, but it's not constant. Expect short, intense downpours in the late afternoon, usually lasting an hour or two. The upside: lush green landscapes, lower prices, and fewer crowds. Hotel rates can drop as much as 50% compared to peak season. Just carry a compact umbrella and plan indoor activities like the Grand Palace or a cooking class for the afternoons.

Festivals and Events Worth Planning Around

If you want to experience Bangkok's culture, plan around its festivals. Loy Krathong (usually in November) sees thousands of floating lanterns on the river. Chinese New Year (January or February) brings vibrant parades in Chinatown. And Songkran in April is a must for party lovers. Each festival draws big crowds, so book early and expect higher prices during those specific dates.

Budget Tips by Season

To save money, aim for the shoulder months of March or October. Flights from the US to Bangkok can drop to $700 round-trip in these months, compared to $1,200 in December. Use fare comparison sites like Skyscanner or Google Flights and set price alerts. For hotels, consider staying in the Sukhumvit area—it's central and has more budget options than riverside hotels.

What to Pack (and What to Skip)

No matter when you go, pack light, breathable clothing—cotton or linen. Skip heavy jeans; you'll regret them in the heat. Always bring a light rain jacket or umbrella if you're visiting between June and October. Comfortable walking shoes are essential for temple visits, but you'll take them off often, so slip-ons are a smart choice.
