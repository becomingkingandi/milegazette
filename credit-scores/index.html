What a credit-score range means

Consumer credit scores commonly summarize information in a credit report. Different lenders may use different bureaus, score versions and decision rules, so the score a consumer sees may not be the score used for an application.

How utilization works

Utilization may be calculated for each revolving account and across accounts. The balance reported to a bureau can differ from the current balance. Paying on time and avoiding unnecessary debt are more durable practices than attempting to optimize for a single snapshot.

Factors beyond a score

Issuers may consider payment history, account age, recent inquiries, income, housing costs, outstanding debt and their own underwriting policies. Public “recommended score” ranges are estimates, not issuer guarantees.

Before applying for a card

  • Review your reports and dispute factual errors through the appropriate bureau.
  • Confirm the annual fee and current issuer terms.
  • Consider whether the rewards match spending you already plan to do.
  • Avoid spending more solely to earn rewards or a welcome offer.
