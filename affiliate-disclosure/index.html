Transparency

Affiliate disclosure.

How commercial relationships may support Mile Gazette—and what they do not control.

How referral links work

Mile Gazette may receive compensation when a reader follows a clearly identified referral link and completes a qualifying action. Using a referral link should not increase the reader’s price, but offer terms can change and must be confirmed with the provider.

Editorial independence

Compensation does not determine our conclusions, methodology or description of a product’s drawbacks. We do not promise that every available product is covered. Commercial relationships and editorial evaluation are treated separately.

How compensated links are labeled

Pages containing compensated links will display a disclosure near the relevant comparison or call to action. Eligible outbound links use appropriate sponsored-link attributes. A button is not an endorsement or an approval guarantee.

Accuracy and applications

Card terms, fees, rewards and eligibility requirements change frequently. Readers should verify the current offer on the provider’s website before applying. Mile Gazette is not a lender and does not make credit decisions.

Current status

Referral components may appear before commercial partnerships are active, but they will link only to Mile Gazette editorial material until an approved partner URL is installed.
