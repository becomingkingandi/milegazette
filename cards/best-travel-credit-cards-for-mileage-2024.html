5 Best Travel Credit Cards for Mileage in 2024: Real Rewards, Real Routes

Stop guessing which card earns the most miles. Here are the 5 cards I've tested on actual trips, with fees, bonuses, and routes that actually work.

5 min read Travel Cards

Why Your Card Choice Matters More Than You Think

I've flown over 50,000 miles in the past year using points from just two cards. The difference between a good card and a great one? About $600 in value per trip. I learned this the hard way after wasting a year with a card that earned 1x points on everything. Now, I only recommend cards that earn at least 2x miles per dollar on travel and dining.

Chase Sapphire Preferred: The Workhorse

Annual fee: $95 (waived first year). Sign-up bonus: 60,000 points after $4,000 spend in 3 months. Transfer partners include United, Southwest, and Hyatt. I used this card to book a round-trip flight to Tokyo for 45,000 points (valued at $675). Best for: flexible travelers who want to transfer points to multiple airlines.

Capital One Venture X: The Premium Option That Pays for Itself

Annual fee: $395. Sign-up bonus: 75,000 miles after $4,000 spend in 3 months. Includes a $300 annual travel credit and 10,000 anniversary bonus miles. Net effective fee: -$5 if you use the credits. I redeemed 50,000 miles for a flight to London (worth $500). Best for: frequent flyers who want lounge access and free checked bags.

American Express Gold: Best for Dining and Groceries

Annual fee: $250. Sign-up bonus: 60,000 Membership Rewards points after $6,000 spend in 6 months. Earn 4x points at restaurants and U.S. supermarkets (up to $25,000 per year). Transfer to Delta, British Airways, or Air France. I earned 12,000 points in 3 months just from groceries. Best for: foodies who fly Delta or partner airlines.

Citi Premier: The Underrated Powerhouse

Annual fee: $95. Sign-up bonus: 60,000 ThankYou points after $4,000 spend in 3 months. Earn 3x on travel, gas, and supermarkets. Transfer partners include Turkish Airlines and Singapore Airlines. I booked a business-class flight to Europe for 45,000 points via Turkish (cash price: $1,200). Best for: travelers who want premium redemptions on lesser-known routes.

Practical Tips for Maximizing Your Miles

Always check transfer partner rates before booking directly. For example, transferring Chase points to United often yields 1.5x more value than using the Chase portal. Set a calendar reminder to use annual credits (like the Venture X $300 credit) within 12 months. And never carry a balance—interest rates on these cards average 20-25%, which wipes out any rewards.

Which Card Should You Get First?

Start with the Chase Sapphire Preferred if you're new to travel cards. It's simple, low fee, and earns transferable points. For experienced travelers who fly 4+ times a year, the Capital One Venture X pays for itself. Avoid applying for more than 2 cards in 6 months—it hurts your credit score. Pick one, use it for 6 months, then add another.
