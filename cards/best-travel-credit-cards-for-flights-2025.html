5 Travel Credit Cards That Actually Save You Money on Flights in 2025

I've tested 20+ travel cards over the last year. Here are the five that put the most money back in your pocket for your next trip.

5 min read Travel Cards

Why Your Current Card Is Costing You

If you're using a standard cash-back card for flights, you're leaving money on the table. The average traveler spends $1,200 per year on airfare. With a dedicated travel card, you can earn 2x to 5x miles per dollar—that's up to 6,000 miles annually just on flights. Over three years, that’s a free round-trip to Europe.

Chase Sapphire Preferred: The All-Rounder

Annual fee: $95. Welcome bonus: 60,000 points after spending $4,000 in 3 months. That's worth $750 toward travel via Chase Ultimate Rewards. You earn 5x on travel purchased through Chase, 3x on dining, and 2x on all other travel. Plus, points transfer 1:1 to partners like United and Hyatt. I've used this card for 10+ trips. It pays for itself.

Capital One Venture X: Premium Without the Sting

Annual fee: $395. But here's the trick: you get a $300 annual travel credit and 10,000 bonus miles ($100) every anniversary. Net effective cost: -$5. Welcome bonus: 75,000 miles after spending $4,000 in 3 months. Earn 10x on hotels and rental cars booked through Capital One Travel, 5x on flights. Lounge access included—Priority Pass and Capital One Lounges. I've saved $200+ on lounge visits alone.

American Express Gold: Best for Foodies Who Fly

Annual fee: $250. Welcome bonus: 60,000 points after spending $6,000 in 6 months. Where this card shines: 4x on restaurants (including takeout) and U.S. supermarkets, 3x on flights booked directly. Also, $120 dining credit and $120 Uber Cash annually. If you eat out twice a week, you'll earn 10,000+ points per year. I used it for a trip to Tokyo—got a free business-class upgrade on ANA.

Citi Premier: The Underrated Workhorse

Annual fee: $95. Welcome bonus: 60,000 points after spending $4,000 in 3 months. Earn 3x on air travel, hotels, gas stations, and restaurants—a rare combo. Points transfer 1:1 to 15+ partners, including Avianca LifeMiles and Turkish Airlines. At 60,000 points, you can book a round-trip to Europe on Turkish for 45,000 miles. I booked a $1,200 flight for $150 in fees.

Bilt Rewards Mastercard: For Renters Who Travel

Annual fee: $0. Yes, zero. Earn 1x on rent (up to 100,000 points per year) and 2x on travel, 3x on dining. Welcome bonus: 80,000 points after spending $5,000 in 5 months. Points transfer to United, Hyatt, and 10+ partners. If you pay $1,500 rent monthly, that's 18,000 points annually—enough for a domestic round-trip. No other card does this.

How to Pick the Right Card for Your Next Trip

Start with your biggest expense. If you fly United or Southwest often, Chase Sapphire Preferred is your best bet. If you're loyal to Delta or don't mind transfer partners, Capital One Venture X gives you lounge access and negative net fee. For renters, Bilt is a no-brainer. Apply for one card at a time—multiple hard inquiries can hurt your credit score. And always pay the balance in full. Interest charges will wipe out any miles you earn.
